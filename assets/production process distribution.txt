Section 1: Yarn Preparation
Is section mein pehle 3 steps combine karein.
Step 1: Yarn Winding
Raw yarn is wound onto cones for smooth production and balanced yarn flow.
Step 2: Yarn Twisting
Yarn is twisted to improve strength, smoothness and durability.
Step 3: Yarn Steaming
Twisted yarn is steamed to set the twist and improve softness and stability.

Section 2: Weaving Preparation
Is mein step 4 and 5 rakhein.
Step 4: Warping & Bobbin Preparation
Yarn is prepared as warp and wound onto bobbins for shuttle weaving.
Step 5: Reed Drawing
Warp yarn is passed through the reed in proper sequence to maintain spacing and fabric width.

Section 3: Fabric Weaving
Is mein main manufacturing step show karein.
Step 6: Power Loom Weaving
Prepared yarn is set on power loom machines where warp and weft threads are interlaced to create fabric.

Section 4: Checking & Finishing
Is mein steps 7, 8, 9 combine karein.
Step 7: Fabric Checking
Fabric is checked for weaving faults, stains, holes, thread lines and surface quality.
Step 8: Fabric Washing
Fabric is washed to remove dust, oil marks and processing particles.
Step 9: Fabric Calendering
Fabric is passed through calendering rollers for smoothness, fall and surface finish.

Section 5: Measuring, Packing & Dispatch
Is mein last 3 steps combine karein.
Step 10: Fabric Measuring
Fabric length and width are measured carefully before packing.
Step 11: Fabric Packing
Fabric is packed with protective wrapping to keep it clean and safe.
Step 12: Fabric Dispatch
Packed orders are dispatched according to customer requirements.
